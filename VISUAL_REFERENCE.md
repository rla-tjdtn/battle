## Locked targets
- Perspective quarter-view, yaw ~45°, pitch ~60°
- Outdoor char screen height ~8–10%
- Warm day lighting, soft shadows
- Dense props (not empty green plane)
- Roof fade local-only; gameplay LOS separate
